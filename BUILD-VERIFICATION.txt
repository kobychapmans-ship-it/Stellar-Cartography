Design change
=============
The movable 3D globe is retired. Solid planets and true moons use fixed rendered survey portraits with procedural continents, oceans or world-type equivalents, biome terrain, topographic relief, bright territory boundaries, settlement markers and selectable satellite close-ups. The legacy planetary data sheet, Stars of Inequity survey, claims, territories, inhabitants, resources, environment rules, settlements and actions remain below.
